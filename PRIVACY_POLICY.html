Jenga Barcode Scanner
Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Last updated 10 October 2026

Your camera images and scan history stay on your device. Jenga Barcode Scanner has no accounts, adverts, analytics or tracking, and does not upload camera frames or saved scans.

Who provides the app?

Jenga Barcode Scanner is provided by George Miller, Jenga-Code. This policy covers version 1.0 of the Android application with package name com.jengacode.barcodescanner.

Camera access and image imports

Camera permission lets you preview and scan barcodes and QR codes. Frames are decoded on your phone and discarded after processing; they are not recorded or sent to a server. If you choose an image through Android's document picker, the app reads that selected image locally to look for a barcode. It does not need access to your whole photo library and does not retain a copy of the imported image.

Local scan history and settings

Scans you save, or scans accepted in continuous mode, are stored in the app's local database. Records contain the decoded value, barcode format, time, content classification and any note you add. Decoded content may itself contain personal information, such as a contact or Wi-Fi password. Treat your saved history and exports accordingly.

Preferences such as theme, sound, vibration, default camera and duplicate cooldown are stored locally. Android backup is disabled for this application. The app does not operate a remote database or sync service.

Optional external actions

Copying places a value on Android's clipboard. Sharing passes the selected text or generated image to the app you choose. Searching opens your browser and sends the scanned value to Google Search. Website, email, dialler and contact actions open the appropriate external app only after you choose and confirm them. Wi-Fi details are displayed for manual use; the app does not automatically connect to a network.

External apps, websites, clipboard behaviour and document providers are governed by their own privacy practices. Choosing a cloud document provider for an export may upload the exported file through that provider. Jenga Barcode Scanner itself does not upload it to a developer-controlled service.

Generated barcodes and exports

Barcode images are generated locally. Save and export operations use Android's system document picker so you choose the destination. Image sharing creates a temporary PNG in the app's cache and grants the chosen receiving app access to that image. Exported CSV and JSON files include all history records and notes. Copies saved or shared outside the app can remain even after you clear history or uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app requests Camera for scanning and Vibration for optional scan feedback. It does not request Internet, contacts, telephone-call, location or broad storage/photo-library permissions. A dialler action does not place a call automatically, and the contact editor lets you review a contact before saving it.

Retention and deletion

Local history is retained until you delete a record, clear history, clear the application's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Notes are removed with their record. You can export before deletion. Clear History requires confirmation. Android may remove cached generated images; clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the app's cache and settings. Deleting inside this app cannot delete copies held by a sharing recipient, browser or external file provider.

Security and collection

No user information or scans are collected remotely by the developer in this version. Local data is held in Android's app storage sandbox; it is not additionally encrypted by this app. Protect your phone with a device lock and avoid sharing sensitive codes. There are no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Changes and contact

Future features may require this policy to change. The policy and store declarations will be updated to reflect any new data handling before release. For support or privacy questions, use the contact details published on the Jenga-Code website.
