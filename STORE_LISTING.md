# Editable Google Play listing

## Name
Jenga Barcode Scanner

## Short description
A fast, private barcode and QR code scanner for Android.

## Full description

Scan everyday barcodes and QR codes with a simple camera interface. Jenga Barcode Scanner decodes on your phone, so scanning works without an internet connection.

POINT AND SCAN
Use your camera to read retail barcodes, QR codes, Code 128 and other supported formats. Adjust zoom, tap to focus, turn on the torch where supported, or scan a selected image. Choose single scanning, continuous scanning or QR-only mode.

KEEP USEFUL RESULTS
Save scans in local history, add notes, search and filter previous results, and export your history as CSV or JSON. Copy or share a result when you need it. You control when to clear your saved scans.

MAKE YOUR OWN CODES
Generate QR codes and supported one-dimensional barcodes from valid text or numbers. Preview the image, choose dimensions, then save a PNG or share it with another app.

PRIVACY BUILT IN
Camera frames and selected images are processed on-device. No accounts, adverts, analytics or tracking. Scan history is stored locally. Optional searches, sharing and website actions open external apps only when you choose them.

SIMPLE CONTROLS
Four clear sections: Scanner, Generate, History and Settings. Choose a dark, light or system theme and control scan sound and vibration.

Supports scanning EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, UPC-E, Code 128, Code 39, Code 93, ITF, Codabar, QR Code, Data Matrix, PDF417 and Aztec. Camera features depend on your phone. QR codes containing Wi-Fi details are displayed for manual connection. External searches require your browser's internet connection. Product names and prices are not provided by the app.

Developed by Jenga-Code.

## Support page draft

Jenga Barcode Scanner support

If scanning is difficult, improve lighting, fit the whole code in the camera view and tap to focus. Enable Camera under Android Settings > Apps > Jenga Barcode Scanner > Permissions if permission was denied. You can also scan a selected image.

History stays on your phone. Export a copy before deleting it. To remove all app data, use Android Settings > Apps > Jenga Barcode Scanner > Storage > Clear data. Saved files and copies shared with other apps remain outside the application.

For a problem report, provide your phone model, Android version and the steps that produced the issue. Avoid sending private barcode contents or passwords. Add your verified, monitored support email before publishing this page or store listing.
